# Monetization Plan

## Positioning

Sell the product as a seller productivity tool that saves time, reduces listing mistakes, and improves listing quality. The clearest initial audience is small to mid-sized eBay sellers who already have enough listing volume for automation to be valuable but do not have custom internal tools.

## Pricing Model

Start with a freemium subscription model:

- Free: limited listing analysis or a small monthly usage allowance.
- Pro: paid monthly plan for individual sellers with higher usage, batch workflows, templates, exports, and priority support.
- Business: higher-priced plan for power sellers with multiple stores, team access, advanced reporting, and higher usage limits.

Avoid taking a percentage of seller revenue at launch unless the product directly proves measurable incremental sales. Sellers are more likely to accept a predictable subscription for productivity tooling.

## Suggested Price Tests

- Free: $0 with limited monthly usage.
- Pro: $19 to $39 per month for individual sellers.
- Business: $79 to $199 per month depending on store count, team seats, and usage.
- Annual plans: two months free in exchange for upfront payment.

Treat these as test ranges, not final pricing. Adjust after measuring activation, repeated usage, support load, and willingness to pay.

## Upgrade Triggers

- Batch processing beyond the free monthly limit.
- Saved templates and reusable listing rules.
- Advanced pricing, fee, and profitability insights.
- Export history and audit logs.
- Team access and multiple eBay account support.
- Priority support.

## Acquisition Channels

- Public download page with a focused demo of the primary workflow.
- eBay seller communities and educational content.
- YouTube demos showing before/after listing workflows.
- SEO pages for specific seller tasks such as listing optimization, item specifics cleanup, price research, and bulk editing.
- Affiliate or partner channels only after compliance review.

## Metrics

- Activation: percentage of users who complete one successful listing workflow.
- Time to value: minutes from install to first useful output.
- Retention: weekly active sellers and repeat workflow usage.
- Conversion: free-to-paid rate by usage level.
- Expansion: additional stores, seats, or higher usage tiers.
- Support burden: tickets per active paid account.
- Reliability: error rate for eBay API calls and import/export workflows.

## Compliance and Risk

- Review eBay developer and marketplace policies before launch.
- Do not imply official eBay endorsement unless a formal partnership exists.
- Avoid storing unnecessary buyer, order, or seller account data.
- Make data deletion and account disconnect paths clear.
- Disclose automation behavior plainly and require user confirmation before destructive listing changes.
- Keep billing, cancellation, refund, and privacy language visible before purchase.
