# Pricing

Pricing uses persisted evidence and never treats active asking prices as sold values.

Supported evidence classifications:

- Verified own sale
- Authorized sold comparable
- User-imported sold comparable
- Active listing
- Catalog estimate
- AI estimate
- Manual value

The pricing service calculates low, median, average, trimmed average, high, quick-sale price, market price, premium price, auction start, estimated fees, shipping, estimated net, comparable count, and confidence.
